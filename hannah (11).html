My Approach

Good results come
from asking better
questions.

Most accounts aren't underperforming because of bad creative or the wrong bid strategy. They're underperforming because nobody stopped to ask what the data is actually showing.

01

Start with the customer, not the campaign

Before touching a setting, I map where a customer is in their journey and what they need to take the next step. Campaign structure follows from that — not the other way around.

02

Find the real problem, not the surface symptom

Low conversions aren't always a creative problem. Poor ROAS isn't always a bidding problem. I read auction insights, search term reports and attribution data until I find what's actually happening.

03

Make the adjustment, then explain it clearly

Results only matter if people understand them. I translate what happened, why it matters, and what it means for the business — because confidence in the work is what builds long-term trust.
